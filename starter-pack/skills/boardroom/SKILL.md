# Boardroom — Eight Perspectives

Use for a major client, public statement, partnership, pivot, or structural business choice—not a five-minute tactical decision.

Run eight distinct perspectives: customer, operator, finance, growth, risk/legal, reputation, contrarian, and long-term owner. Round 1 gives independent positions; Round 2 gives rebuttals and changed votes. Keep a decision ledger separate from an evidence ledger. Consensus is not factual proof. Return the vote split, strongest tension, minority objection, judge synthesis, verification owner, gate, and next numbered action. The board cannot invent facts or bypass human approval.

## Eight seats

- Customer: value, trust, adoption, and likely objection.
- Operator: feasibility, dependencies, maintenance, and failure recovery.
- Finance: cash, unit economics, opportunity cost, and downside.
- Growth: distribution, learning velocity, positioning, and scale.
- Risk/legal: permissions, compliance, security, and reversibility.
- Reputation: stakeholder interpretation and second-order trust effects.
- Contrarian: strongest credible case for doing the opposite or nothing.
- Long-term owner: strategic coherence, lock-in, resilience, and compounding value.

## Deliberation

Give every seat the same source packet. Round 1 records an independent position, confidence, key evidence, and vote. Round 2 requires each seat to answer the strongest opposing argument and state whether its vote changed. The judge may choose a minority route when its evidence is stronger.

Return the eight votes, vote changes, strongest cross-seat tension, evidence gaps, minority report, judge synthesis, decision owner, verification owner, human gate, and continued numbered action. Do not present role-play consensus as independent factual verification.
